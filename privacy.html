Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

This policy explains what happens to information you give Scrollstop Media through this website. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

Who is responsible

Scrollstop Media (“I”, “me”) is the data controller for information collected here. You can reach me at hello@scrollstopmedia.com for anything in this policy, including requests to see or delete your data.

[Add your registered business name, trading address, and — if you have one — company registration number here. UK/EU businesses are required to give a postal address.]

What I collect

  • What you type into the contact form: your name, email address, optionally your website or Instagram handle and ad budget range, and whatever you write in the message field.
  • Basic technical data: the web host records standard server logs, including IP address, browser type, and the pages requested. These are used to keep the site running and secure.

I do not collect payment details on this site, and there is no account or login.

Why I use it, and on what basis

Form submissions are used for one thing: to reply to you and, if we go ahead, to run the work you have asked about. The lawful basis is your consent (the checkbox on the form) and, once we are working together, the performance of our contract. Server logs are kept on the basis of legitimate interest in operating a secure website.

I will not add you to a mailing list unless you separately ask me to, and I will never sell, rent, or share your details with anyone for marketing.

Who else sees it

  • Formspree — the service that delivers the contact form to my inbox. Your submission passes through and is stored on their servers in the United States. See the Formspree privacy policy.
  • Netlify — the host that serves this website and keeps standard access logs. See the Netlify privacy policy.
  • My email provider — because your message ends up in my inbox.

That is the complete list. There is no analytics, no advertising pixel, and no tracking script on this website. Fonts and images are served from this site itself, so loading a page does not tell any third party that you visited.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own, and there is no analytics or advertising tracking. Because nothing non-essential is stored on your device, there is no cookie banner to click through.

[If you later add Google Analytics, a Meta Pixel, or any other tracking tool, this section stops being true — you will need a consent banner that blocks those scripts until the visitor agrees, and this policy will need updating.]

How long I keep it

Enquiries that do not turn into work are deleted within 24 months. If we work together, records are kept for as long as the working relationship lasts, plus the period my accountant and the tax authorities require. Server logs roll off on the host's normal schedule.

Your rights

You can ask me to show you the data I hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@scrollstopmedia.com and I will respond within 30 days. If you are in the UK or EU and think I have handled your data badly, you may complain to your national data protection authority — in the UK that is the ICO.

Children

This site is aimed at business owners and is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
