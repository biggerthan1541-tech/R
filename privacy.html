Last updated: 20 August 2026

This policy explains what happens to information you give Scrollstop Media through this website. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

What I collect

  • What you type into the contact form: your name, email address, optionally your website or Instagram handle and ad budget range, and whatever you write in the message field.
  • Basic technical data: the web host records standard server logs, including IP address, browser type, and the pages requested. These are used to keep the site running and secure.

I do not collect payment details on this site, and there is no account or login.

Why I use it

Form submissions are used for one thing: to reply to you, and — if we go ahead — to run the work you asked about. Server logs exist to keep the site online and secure.

I will not add you to a mailing list unless you separately ask me to, and I will never sell, rent, or trade your details. I do not sell personal information to anyone, for any purpose.

Who else sees it

  • Formspree — the service that delivers the contact form to my inbox. Your submission passes through and is stored on their servers in the United States. See the Formspree privacy policy.
  • Netlify — the host that serves this website and keeps standard access logs. See the Netlify privacy policy.
  • Google (Gmail) — my email inbox. Your message is delivered there and stored on Google's servers. See the Google privacy policy.

That is the complete list. There is no analytics, no advertising pixel, and no tracking script on this website. Fonts and images are served from this site itself, so loading a page does not tell any third party that you visited.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own, and there is no analytics or advertising tracking. Because nothing non-essential is stored on your device, there is no cookie banner to click through.

[If you later add Google Analytics, a Meta Pixel, or any other tracking tool, this section stops being true — you will need a consent banner that blocks those scripts until the visitor agrees, and this policy will need updating.]

How long I keep it

Enquiries that do not turn into work are deleted within 24 months. If we work together, records are kept for as long as the working relationship lasts, plus the period required for tax and accounting purposes. Server logs roll off on the host's normal schedule.

Your choices

Wherever you live, you can ask me to show you what I hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email robemma4278@gmail.com and I will deal with it within 30 days. There is no charge and I will not treat you differently for asking.

If you are in the UK, EU, or California

This is a US business, but the website is reachable from anywhere, so a few extra rights may apply to you.

  • UK and EU (UK GDPR / GDPR): where these apply, my lawful basis for handling form submissions is your consent — the checkbox on the form — and, once we are working together, performance of our contract. You may withdraw consent at any time, request a copy of your data in a portable format, or object to how it is used. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): I do not sell or share personal information, and I do not use it for cross-context behavioural advertising. You may request access to or deletion of what I hold.

Your information is stored and processed in the United States. If you are contacting me from outside the US, submitting the form means your details are transferred there.

Children

This site is aimed at business owners and is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
