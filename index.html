Why work with someone new

The honest case for hiring me before there's a waiting list.

  • You're not account number forty

    Bigger agencies hand you a junior and a template. My roster is small — by necessity right now, by choice later — so your account gets thought about properly.

  • The rate reflects the risk

    You'd be taking a chance on someone without a public track record. The founding rate is me pricing that honestly instead of pretending it isn't true.
